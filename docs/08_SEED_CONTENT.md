# 08 — Seed Content Plan

The MVP should look useful without requiring hundreds of records.

## Target initial volume

- 30–40 fashion items
- 5 styles
- 20–30 outfits
- 6–10 fashion stories
- 5 color stories / featured colors

During foundation work, use a much smaller representative fixture. Expand only when the domain pages exist.

## Initial styles

1. Modern Classic
2. Quiet Luxury
3. Minimalist
4. Soft Feminine
5. Casual Chic

## Seed content quality rule

Every seed record should demonstrate a real relationship in the domain model. Avoid filler lorem ipsum once the corresponding page is under active development.
